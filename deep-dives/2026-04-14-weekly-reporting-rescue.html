Companion Deep Dive

Weekly Reporting Rescue proof sheet

This is the current best first offer because it is small, believable, and tied to pain Micah already understands deeply: status scramble, inbox digging, and repeated reporting reconstruction.

Current read

Start with a reporting wedge, not a giant transformation pitch.

What the offer is

A small engagement that turns messy status chasing into a repeatable reporting rhythm for operators already buried in email, spreadsheets, and manual reconstruction.

Best-fit buyers

Utility and field-ops subcontractors, construction project teams, and admin-heavy teams drowning in email and spreadsheet reporting.

Why it fits

It is close to Micah's lived pattern-recognition edge and does not require a giant build to prove value.

Deliverables

One reporting template, one intake path, one summary view, and one short SOP.

Expansion path

Inbox-to-tracker cleanup first, Operator HQ or custom dashboard work later.

Build order

  1. Create a one-page proof sheet.
  2. Capture one before-and-after reporting story.
  3. Use one screenshot or mock from Operator HQ as proof support.
  4. List 10 to 15 warm leads by bucket.

Recommended positioning

What to say

I help small operations teams cut reporting scramble without forcing a big software migration.

What not to say

Do not lead with generic AI consulting, vague productivity coaching, or full custom app promises.
