Deep Dive

Pokémon findings should be filtered through Micah's ownership rules, not generic hype.

A good daily cron job is not supposed to throw ROM hacks at Micah blindly. It should understand taste, ownership, legality comfort, and whether a better recommendation includes a legitimate purchase path.

Key behavior

Recommend with context, not just enthusiasm.

Why this matters

The job is not simply to discover a cool fan game. The job is to notice whether Micah would likely enjoy it, check whether it conflicts with his stated ownership preference, and adapt the recommendation accordingly.

Correct pattern

  • Find a game that genuinely fits Micah's taste.
  • Check whether it depends on a base game he owns.
  • If he does not own it, suggest a legitimate purchase path instead of pretending that does not matter.
  • Create a companion page when the recommendation has enough context, links, or decision detail.

Why this is bigger than a report

This is a judgment system. The value comes from matching findings to Micah specifically, respecting known constraints, and doing enough legwork that the next decision becomes easier and more grounded.
